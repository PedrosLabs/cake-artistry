Pricing Guide

A guide to our starting prices. Every cake is bespoke, so get in touch and Victoria will put together a quote for exactly what you have in mind.

Buttercream & Ganache Cakes

All standard single tier cakes have three layers of sponge, in the flavour and fillings of your choice. Each cake can then be finished with a covering of buttercream, or white, milk or dark chocolate ganache.

Starting prices include classic decorations such as drips, basic buttercream toppings and sprinkles. Additional fondant decorations, palette knife painted cakes, heavily decorated cakes and cake toppers carry an additional charge.

Tall cakes have four layers of sponge, giving extra height and effect — serving sizes increase accordingly.

Single Tier

  • Standard 6 inch from £60 Serves 10–14
  • Standard 8 inch from £80 Serves 18–24
  • Tall 6 inch from £70 Serves 16–20
  • Tall 8 inch from £90 Serves 26–30

Tiered Cakes

  • Small 2 Tier from £100 Serves 14–20 4 and 6 inch, three layer sponges
  • Standard 2 Tier from £150 Serves 28–38 6 and 8 inch, three layer sponges

Prices are a starting guide and vary with design and decoration. Send an enquiry for a quote tailored to your celebration.
